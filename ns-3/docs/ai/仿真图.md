## 1、不同信标周期下预测准确度（三个场景都写）

![原文图片1](assets/simulation-figures/image1.png)

Firstly, it can be observed that the accuracy of the prediction gradually decreases with the increase of the period. This trend arises because longer distribution periods result in less accurate CITs obtained by vehicles periodically, thereby reducing the accuracy of predictions derived from historical CITs. Conversely, as the period increases, the number of information interactions between vehicles and RSUs becomes less, resulting in a reduction in the additional overhead incurred. Therefore, a trade-off exists between prediction accuracy and overhead. Notably, even in the case of a long distribution interval (e.g., 4s, which is 40x times the requesting rate), the prediction accuracy can remain high (above 90%), and even higher than 95% when the distribution period is 1s. This shows that our prediction module can greatly improve the accuracy of the collected data and achieve excellent performance. Striking a balance between prediction accuracy and additional overhead, we set the distribution period T = 2s in subsequent experiments.

## 2、NVT、NCT两个表大小，update和registration两个数据包大小、TKAN推断时延

Communication Overhead: Regarding the communication overhead of the algorithm, we introduce a new data structure CIT for each vehicle. The structure of CIT is shown as TABLE II and the size of it is approximately 4B, which is much smaller than the Data packet. In addition, the time interval T for the CITs’ synchronization is also much larger than the user’s request rate. Consequently, the additional communication overhead incurred by CAAF is much smaller than that of the data transmission, and will not affect the data transmission. The computational as well as communication overhead of the algorithm grows linearly with the number of vehicles. Due to the typically limited number of vehicles and content items, the additional overhead incurred by our proposed cacheaware adaptive forwarding strategy remains manageable and acceptable.

Performance under Different Node Densities. Varying the numbers of nodes, we evaluate the performance of each forwarding strategy under different node densities.

描述参考Data Forwarding Scheme for Vehicle Tracking in Named Data Networking，该实验图如下图所示。

In Fig. 9(a), as the number of Consumers increases, the average delay that FN consumes increases significantly, while V-NDN, PCNDN, and VTDF become stable, and the average delay in the VTDF case is the smallest. The reason is that the FN uses the V2V communication mode, and the performance of this method depends on the density of vehicle nodes. The V-NDN, PCNDN, and VTDF techniques use the V2R communication mode, and changes in the number of Consumers have relatively little effect on them. The VTDF uses the Data packet tracking vehicle route recovery method to reduce the loss of Data packets and unnecessary repeated requests for Interest packets. Simultaneously, in the process of data forwarding, the VTDF technique also includes a specific cache method to improve the cache hit rate of the data content, making its performance in terms of the average delay is better than the PCNDN, FN and V-NDN.
